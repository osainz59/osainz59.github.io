About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of the Basque Country in the HiTZ center and IXA group. My research interests are focused on low-resource scenarios and particularly on low-resource Information Extraction.

I obtained my PhD at the University of the Basque Country in 2024, where I worked on zero and few-shot Information Extraction using entailment models and making LLMs learn to follow annotation guidelines.

Research Interests

Low-Resource NLP Information Extraction Large Language Models Cross-lingual Evaluation Basque NLP Data Contamination

Open-Source Projects

GoLLIE

A large language model fine-tuned to follow annotation guidelines, improving zero-shot Information Extraction on schemas it has never seen. Built with HiTZ.

Code Paper Models

Latxa

An open family of language models for Basque (7B-70B), with its pretraining corpus and evaluation suite. Best Resource Paper, ACL 2024.

Code Paper

Ask2Transformers

A framework for zero-shot text classification built on textual entailment models - the basis for much of my early work on zero-shot Information Extraction.
